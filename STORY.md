# State Shift — story bible

## The scientist
**Dr. Kettle** (no first name needed; "they"). A brilliant, sleep-deprived thermodynamicist in Lab 3.
Lab coat, amber goggles pushed up into messy auburn hair, a red "H2O" coffee mug that never leaves their hand.
Kind and curious, a bit chaotic. Talks to their experiments.

## The experiment
**Experiment 47: thermo-responsive water.** One flask of perfectly ordinary H2O, plus a pinch of
*cryo-salt*, a splash of *thermal catalyst* and "one teeny little zap" from the THERMO-REG 9000
(a desk-sized Tesla-coil temperature regulator).

## The accident
At 2:47 AM the regulator hums too hard, arcs a bolt of lightning straight into the flask and blows
the lab's power. Dr. Kettle faints ("...five more minutes..."). In the dark, the glowing water in
flask 47 has its very first thought: *"...huh?"*

## The blob
Forty-seven, a little blob of H2O with big eyes. It never speaks; it reacts with eyes and emote
bubbles (! ? note ... heart). It discovers it can **freeze** (ICE), **melt** (WATER) and **float** (STEAM).
**Motivation:** pure curiosity plus a newborn's need to see what is outside. When the PA announces a
containment breach and the doors seal, it realizes water doesn't need doors, and drips down the drain.

## The ending
It pops out of the rooftop flue into a moonlit night with no ceiling for the first time. It looks up
at the big slow clouds, turns to steam and floats up to join them (the cloud gets its eyes). Then it
rains, gently, over the hills, the lake and one quiet lab.
Next morning Dr. Kettle (bandage on head) finds the empty flask, a wet trail to the drain and rain on
the window, and smiles. Lab log: *"Result: rain. Quite a lot of it. Conclusion: Good for you, little one."*
Then the end screen: the blob sliding out of a pipe toward the lake, its postcard from freedom.

## Voices
- **DR. KETTLE**: warm, mumbly, a little proud of their mistakes.
- **PA**: deadpan corporate safety voice ("Please remain calm. And dry.").
- **LAB LOG / notes**: dry and accidentally fond.
- **Narration**: short, gentle, storybook.
